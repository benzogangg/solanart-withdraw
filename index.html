Withdraw SOL from your old Solanart offers

Unofficial, open-source page, not affiliated with Solanart. When you made an offer on Solanart, its SOL was locked in a separate on-chain account. The Solanart website no longer shows many of these offers, but the program still lets their maker cancel them. This page finds every open offer made by your wallet and sends the same Cancel Bid instruction. The SOL goes to your own wallet, and you sign the transactions yourself.

What exactly you sign

Only instructions of the Solanart program CJsLwbP1iu5DuUikHEJnLfANgKy6stB2uFgvBBHoyxwz, Cancel Bid (data 03), one per offer, up to 8 per transaction. Each one closes an offer account that your wallet created and sends its SOL to your wallet. The small account rent (0.0025 SOL per offer) goes back to the refund address stored in the offer, which is normally your wallet too. There are no token approvals and no transfers to anyone else. Network fee is 0.000005 SOL per transaction. Your wallet shows its own simulation before you sign.

Source code and how to verify it: see the repository this page is served from.
